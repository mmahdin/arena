# Theia visual identity — signal, aperture, horizon

## Core idea

Theia is not represented by a character. The identity is a small act of seeing: a point of light held in the quiet opening of an aperture, with a horizon beneath it. The mark can suggest dawn and vision without depicting a goddess, face, eye, or literal celestial body.

The visual story moves from **noticing → looking closer → finding a line through complexity → opening toward the horizon**. Rings and contours are used as thresholds and fields of attention, not as tech diagrams. Keep the compositions spacious and let one warm point carry the emotional emphasis.

## Mark and palette

- `TheiaMark` is the compact arch / point / split-horizon signature. Use it in monochrome at small sizes; reserve warm amber for the point of focus.
- The bilingual lockup is `public/brand/theia-logo.svg`; the favicon and installed-app icons use the same threshold motif.
- Warm amber (`#e8bd82`) is the signal; bone and quiet stone lines frame it; the existing muted lavender may remain as a secondary atmospheric tint. Keep backgrounds charcoal or the existing warm light neutral.
- Prefer fine strokes, low contrast in the outer rings, and a single focal point. No rays, stars, faces, AI nodes, gradients used as decoration, or dense ornament.

## Onboarding sequence

1. **Welcome:** one point appears inside a broad, quiet field.
2. **Simple questions:** the field gains a few measured contours; focus becomes intentional.
3. **Deeper questions:** oblique nested ellipses draw the eye inward.
4. **Knowing:** the point sends a fine path out through layered contours toward open space.
5. **Almost there:** the composition resolves into a complete, balanced field.
6. **Privacy:** the point remains within a calm, open boundary — held, not locked away.
7. **Check-ins:** a set of topographic / orbital contours offers several points of attention without depicting a person or emotion emoji.
8. **Ready:** the contours widen into a clear horizon; light becomes outward-facing.

The screens retain their existing layout and typography; the illustration area is the narrative layer. SVG illustrations are intentionally transparent so they sit naturally on the existing light and dark onboarding surfaces.
